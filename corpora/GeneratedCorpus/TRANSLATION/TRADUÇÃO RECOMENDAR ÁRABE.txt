INTĀJ AZ-ZAYT

* zayt zaytūn munaqqā (refined): Yutamm intājuhu min tanqiyat az-zayt al-bikr, alladhī yaḥtawī ‘alā nisbat ḥumūḍa murtafi‘a wa wujūd ‘uyūb yajibu izālatuhā athnāʾ ‘amaliyyat at-tanqiya. Yumkin khalṭuhu ma‘a az-zayt al-bikr.

* zayt zaytūn bikr: Yuḥṣal ‘alayhi bi-ṭuruq mīkānīkiyya. Wa-tab‘an li-nisbat al-ḥumūḍa fī al-muntaj an-nātij, yumkin taṣnīf hādhā az-zayt ka-naw‘ fāʾiq (extra), aw bikr, aw ‘ādī. Az-zayt al-bikr la yatijāwaz ḥadd al-ḥumūḍa al-aqṣā 2%.

* zayt zaytūn bikr fāʾiq (extra virgin): Lā yajūz an tatajāwaz al-ḥumūḍa 0.8% (ḥamḍ al-ūlīyik), kamā lā yajūz an yaḥtawī ‘alā ‘uyūb. Al-jihah allatī tunaẓẓim hādhā wa tuḥaddid ayy al-‘uyūb yutamm taṣnīfuhā hiya al-Majlis ad-Duwalī li-z-Zuyūt (COI).

* zayt zaytūn ‘ādī: Yuḥṣal ‘alayhi min khalṭ zayt lampante, ghayr aṣ-ṣāliḥ lil-istihlāk, ba‘da i‘ādat tadwīrihi bi-ṭuruq fīziyāʾiyya-kīmiyāʾiyya, ma‘a zayt bikr wa zayt bikr fāʾiq. Az-zayt al-‘ādī lā yūjad lahu tanẓīm muḥaddad.

NĀṢĀʾIḤ LIL-ISTIHLĀK:

‘Inda istihlāk al-muntaj, yunṣaḥ bi-t-taḥaqqiq min nisbat al-ḥumūḍa wa tārīkh aṣ-ṣalāḥiyya.

Hiya tumaththil al-Markaz ar-Rūḥī (al-Markaz al-Isbīrītī) alladhī istaqbal al-muʾassasa (dār ar-ri‘āya).

Lākin al-Ittiḥād ar-Rūḥī al-Baladī, khilāl fatrat istikmāl al-awrāq, wa li-annahu lam yakun huwa al-mukhtār li-talqqī dār ar-ri‘āya, wa bi-musā‘adat muḥāmin rūḥī, yuḥāwil an yaj‘al dār ar-ri‘āya al-madhkūra tuntaqal ilayhim.

Lam yakun hunāka ayy ittifāq ‘alā dhālik.
