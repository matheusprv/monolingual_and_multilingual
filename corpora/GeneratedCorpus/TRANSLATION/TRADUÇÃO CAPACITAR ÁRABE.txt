Al muajjan al sukkar (fondant)

Masa' al khayr ya jami'!
Ismi Tatiana, ana 'udwa jadida fi al muntada wa ladayya su'al:
ana uhibb zinat al ka'ik bil muajjan al sukkar kathiran wa da'iman tammannayt an ata'allamahu, lakin intaqaltu ila al Wilayat al Muttahida. Abhathu 'an dawraat huna lakin la a'rif kayfa yusammuna al muajjan al sukkar. Bahathtu fi mawaqi' kathira wa wajadtu khamsat anwa' aw akthar min anwa' mukhtalifa. Hal ahad ya'rif kayfa yusamma haadha al muajjan huna? A'rif anna kull ma yustakhdam li tazyin al ka'ik yusamma "icing" aw shay' akhar, lakin al muajjan al sukkar lam ajidhu.
In kana ahad yastati' musaa'adati fa sa'akun shaakiratan jiddan. Shukran jazeelan lil jami'!

RE: Al muajjan al sukkar

Marhaba Tati, ahlan wa sahlan biki fi al muntada!
Lil muajjan al sukkar asma' 'adida. A'taqid anna al ism al akthar shuyu'an lada al amrikiyin huwa "rolled fondant". Kama anna hunaak "gum paste". Amma al baritaniyun fa yufadhdilun lafthat "sugarcraft". Ibhathi 'an hadhihi al asma' fi Google wa sa tajideen 'adadan la yuhsa min al mawaqi' allati tuqaddim ma'lumat wa dawraat wa mawaad wa kathiran ghayr dhalik.

Risala maftuha
Al naziyya kashafat lana aqbaha wajh fi al insaan — dhalika al wajh al muzlim aladhi tuhaawil al insaaniyya nasyaan.
Al naziyya fi siyasatiha tu'tabar namuthaajan lada ba'd baahithi al taareekh, ghayr anna tatarrufaha marfud min qibalihim anfusihim. Al hukuma al naziyya anqadhat almaniya min al azma al kaabila allati kanat taghraqu fiha wa a'taatha wujhat nadar wa amal. Ghayr anna dhalika al amal istu'mila min qibal ashkhas su'aa' al niyya li khida' al naas wa qatlihim. Ana lan ukhin min idanat al jara'im allati irtakabathal almaniyya al naaziyya. Adinu kull jarima didd al insaniyya. Lakin min al haqq an aqula inna ana — wa ana rajulun nasha'a fi kanaf al thaqafa al sharqiyya — axin al ramz aladhi tuthi' allahu al swastika. Khudh haadha al mithal: nadi Vasku da Gama al brazili yasta'milu ramz al saleeb al malteez — ramz musta'mal mundhu mi'aat al sineen. Anaa mushaaji' Flamengu — fa hal yajib an akraha dhalika al ramz li mujarrad ann nadi Vasku da Gama qad tabannahu? Haadha la ya'qul abadan. Al 'aaqil man ya'rif al fasl bayn al umur.
