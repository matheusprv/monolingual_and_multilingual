Fan tang / Gum paste

Da jia wan shang hao!
Wo shi Tati, lun tan xin ren, you ge wen ti xiang qing jiao da jia:
Wo hen ai yong fan tang zuo dan gao zhuang shi, yi zhi hen xiang xue, dan wo qian bu jiu ban dao Meiguo le. Zai zhao xue xi ke cheng, que bu zhi dao Meiguo ren ba fan tang jiao zuo shen me. Wo zai hen duo wang zhan shang cha le cha, fa xian zhi shao you wu liu zhong bu tong de chan pin. You ren zhi dao zai zhe li fan tang tong chang jiao zuo shen me ma? Wo zhi dao zhuang shi dan gao yong de dong xi yi ban tong cheng "icing", dan jiu shi zhao bu dao "fan tang" zhe ge dong xi.
Ru guo you ren neng bang bang wo jiu hao le, xian xie da jia!

Gong kai xin
Na cui zhan lu le ren lei zui hei an de yi mian — na ge ren men yi zhi shi tu wang ji de hei an yi mian.
Na cui de zheng zhi li nian bei bu fen li shi xue jia shi zuo mou zhong can kao mo shi, dan qi ji duan zhu yi ze bei zhe xie xue zhe ben ren suo fu ren. Na cui zheng quan shi de guo guo cong kun jing zhong jie tuo chu lai, bing wei qi dai lai le xi wang. Dan zhe fen xi wang que bei you xin huai gui zhi de ren li yong, yong lai mengpian he sha ren. Wo jue dui bu zhi chi na cui de guo suo fan xia de yi qie zui xing. Wo fan dui yi qie wei hu ren lei de zui xing. Ran er bu ke fou ren de shi, wo — yi ge shou dong fang wen hua xun tao de ren — que shi xin shang wan zi zhe ge fu hao suo bao han de yi yi. Ju ge li zi: Vasku (Vasco da Gama) qiu dui xian zai shi yong de shi Ma er ta shi zi biao zhi — yi ge yi jing shi yong le ji bai nian de qi hao. Ni kan, wo shi Flamengo qiu mi — nan dao jiu yin wei Vasku cai yong le zhe ge biao zhi, wo jiu gai tao yan ta ma? Jue dui bu. Yao xue hui ba bu tong de shi qing fen kai lai kan.
