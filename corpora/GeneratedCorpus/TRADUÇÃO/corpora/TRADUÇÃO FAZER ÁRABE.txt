Al-mu'tamar al-awwal lil-tarbiyya al-khassa sayuqaam fi yawmay 19 wa 20 Agushtus.
Al-tasjīl maftūḥ ma'a khasm dilya rannikh uchastnikov.

Fi yawmay 19 wa 20 Agushtus, sayastaḍīf al-ḥarām al-jāmiʿī Anália Franco al-mu'tamar al-awwal lil-tarbiyya al-khassa — PROESP, bi-fokus ʿalā al-mawḍūʿ al-ra'īsī: "Munāqashat al-ḥudūd wa al-imkāniyyāt fī al-ʿamal al-tadrīsī".
Hādhā al-ḥadath mawjjah lil-mutakhaṣṣiṣīn al-ʿāmilīn fī majāl al-idmāj al-madrasī lil-ṭullāb dhawī al-iḥtiyājāt al-khāṣṣa, wa-kadhalika lil-bāḥithīn fī majāl al-tarbiyya al-khāṣṣa. Sayuwaffir al-mu'tamar lil-mushārikīn manassa lil-tafkīr wa al-niqāsh ḥawla al-qāḍāyā al-muʿāṣira, maʿa al-tarkīz ʿalā al-dawr al-asāsī lil-firaq al-mutaʿaddidat al-khaṣāṣāt. Inna khibratahum wa-maʿrifatahum tatashakkal ʿinda bawtāqat al-ʿamal al-ʿamalī lil-muʿallimīna wa al-daʿm al-pedagogī al-mutakhaṣṣiṣ al-lāzim lidāmān taʿlīm ʿālī al-jawda.
Yatimm tanẓīm hādhā al-mu'tamar fī iṭār Barnāmaj al-Tarbiyya al-Khāṣṣa (PROESP) bi-daʿm min Wakālat al-ʿAlāqāt al-ʿĀmmah wa al-Taʿlīm al-Mustamirr fī Jāmiʿat Cruzeiro do Sul. Hadaf al-mashrūʿ huwa taʿzīz al-rabiṭ bayna al-baḥth wa al-tadrīs wa al-mumarasa al-ʿamaliyya fī majāl al-taʿlīm al-shāmil. Yuwaffir al-barnāmaj daʿman pedagogiyyan lil-shabāb wa al-bālighīn dhawī al-iʿāqāt (al-dhihniyya, al-samʿiyya, al-ḥarakiyya, wa al-baṣariyya) wa-li-ʿā'ilātihim. Wa-hādhā yusāʿid ʿalā idmājihim fī al-sūq al-ʿamalī, wa-yuftāḥ lahum al-wuṣūl ilā al-khayrāt al-ijtimāʿiyya wa al-thaqāfiyya, wa-yarfaʿu min taqdīr al-dhāt, wa-yunammī al-istiqlāliyya, wa-yu'akkidu ʿalā ahammiyyat idmājihim al-kāmil fī al-mujtamaʿ.

AL-USBŪʿ AL-ḤĀDĪ WA AL-THALĀTHŪN LIL-MUTARJIM — MIN 26 ILĀ 30 SIBTAMBIR 2011

Inna Usbūʿ al-Mutarjim huwa ḥadath sanawī taqlīdī yatimm tanẓīmuhu mundhu ʿām 1981 fī Jāmiʿat Wilāyat San-Paulu (UNESP) fī San-Zhoze-du-Riu-Pretu (Wilāyat San-Paulu). Wa-huwa yajmaʿu kibār al-mutakhaṣṣiṣīn, al-mutarjimīn al-mihaniyyīn al-mumarisīn, wa-ṭullāb al-tarjama min mukhtalaf anḥā' al-bilād.
Fī ʿām 2011, wa-fī iṭār al-ijtimāʿ al-ḥādī wa al-thalāthūn, sayakūn Usbūʿ al-Mutarjim fī IBILCE – UNESP mukhaṣṣaṣan li-mawḍūʿ "Al-Tiknūlūjiyā wa al-Tarjama wa al-Thaqāfa". Yaḍaʿu al-munaẓẓimūn naṣba aʿyunihim hadaf tabyīn madā ahammiyyat wa-ʿadam imkāniyyat al-istighnā' fī al-mihna ʿan al-injāzāt al-tiknūlūjiyya li-nihāyat al-qarn al-ʿishrīn — khāṣṣatan, anẓimat dhākirat al-tarjama (TM). Bi-faḍl al-warashāt al-mukaththafa al-latī satustamarr ʿalā madār al-usbūʿ, sayatamakkanu al-mushārikūn min taʿmīq maʿrifatihim bi-l-adawāt al-asāsiyya al-lāzima li-kulli mutarjim muʿāṣir.
Kama sayatānawalu barnāmaj al-usbūʿ al-ḥādī wa al-thalāthūn al-jānib al-thaqāfī ayḍan. La-qad qurrira tanẓīm dawrāt qaṣīra mutaʿaddida ḥawla al-adab, al-rasm, al-mūsīqā, wa al-sīnimā fī siyāq al-lughāt al-ajnabiyya wa al-lughat al-umm ʿalā ḥaddin sawā'.
Sayatimm tawjīh ihtimām khāṣṣ ilā al-ʿamaliyyāt al-thaqāfiyya wa-rabiṭihā al-wathīq bi-l-tarjama min khilāl al-manāhij al-tārīkhiyya, al-lughawiyya, al-siyāsiyya, wa al-ijtimāʿiyya.
Nuriidu an nu'akkida ʿalā qīmat al-daʿā'im al-ra'īsiyya fī al-ʿamal ʿalā ayyi naṣṣ: al-barāʿa al-taqaniyya wa al-khafiyya al-thaqāfiyya al-ʿamīqa, al-latī bi-dūnihā lā yumkinu al-istiʿmāl al-daqīq lil-kalimāt.
Sayatimm nashr al-barnāmaj al-mufaṣṣal fī al-waqt al-qarīb ʿalā mawqiʿinā al-rasmī:

 Riḥlat "Al-Ghāba al-Aṭlasīyya"
DAWRAT AL-ʿAMAL AL-MAYDĀNĪ: Bikūlūjiyā wa-Ḥifẓ al-Ghāba al-Aṭlasīyya
(100 sāʿatin min al-durūs al-naẓariyya wa al-ʿamaliyya)
         Al-Mawsim al-13 — min 21 ilā 31 Yūlyū 2011
         ĀKHIR MAWʿID LIL-TAQDĪM: 18 Yūlyū 2011


Al-Munaẓẓimūn: Al-Majmūʿa al-Bī'iyya "Zelenyy Brazil" / Mu'assasat Diaulas Abreu – Al-Maʿhad al-Fidirālī li-Janūb Sharq Wilāyat Minas-Zherays (IF Sudeste MG) / Majmūʿat al-Baḥth fī Takhṭīṭ wa-Idārat al-Manāṭiq al-Ṭabīʿiyya al-Maḥmīyya – IF Sudeste MG.

Inna dawrat "Bikūlūjiyā wa-Ḥifẓ al-Ghāba al-Aṭlasīyya" hiya tadrīb ʿamalī maydānī yatimm fī iṭār riḥla min al-istishāf. Hādhihi al-riḥla tustamarr li-muʿaddal aḥada ʿashara yawman ʿabra al-maḥmiyyāt, al-hadā'iq al-waṭaniyya, wa al-manāṭiq al-maḥmiyya al-khāṣṣa wa al-baladiyya wa al-iqlīmiyya wa al-fidirāliyya, wa-kadhalika maʿāhid al-baḥth al-ʿilmī fī Wilāyat Parana.
Bi-l-iḍāfa ilā al-ziyārāt al-taqaniyya lil-mawqāʿ, yaḍmumu al-barnāmaj muḥāḍarāt, wa-niqāshāt mihaniyya, wa-tabādulan mukaththafan lil-khibrāt ḥawla qāḍāyā al-bī'a wa-ḥifẓ al-muḥīṭ.
Yuansiqu al-ʿamal al-brūfīsūr Zheraldu Mazhela Morais Salviu, mudarris bikūlūjiyā wa-bī'at ḥifẓ al-anwāʿ fī al-Maʿhad al-Fidirālī li-Janūb Sharq Wilāyat Minas-Zherays (Ḥarām Barbasena), bi-mushārakat brūfīsūrāt madʿuwwīn. Al-Dawra maftūḥa li-kulli man yahtammu bi-hādhā al-majāl mukhliṣan, bi-ghāḍḍi al-naẓar ʿan al-taʿlīm al-asāsī aw al-jinsiyya.
Hunāka 20 maqʿadan lil-mushārikīn al-brazīliyyīn wa al-ajānib al-ladhīna yarghabūna fī taʿmīq dirāsat al-niẓām al-bī'ī lil-Ghāba al-Aṭlasīyya wa al-mashākil al-ra'īsiyya li-ḥifẓihā, bi-mā fī dhālik takhṭīṭ wa-idārat al-manāṭiq al-maḥmiyya ʿalā al-madā al-ṭawīl.
Kama satatānawalu al-dawra maḍāmīn tārīkhiyya, wa al-thaqāfa al-maḥalliyya, wa al-mujtamaʿāt al-taqlīdiyya, wa al-siyāḥa al-bī'iyya wa al-tanmiya al-mustadāma.
Tuʿqadu al-dawra sanawiyyan fī Yanāyir wa Yūlyū mundhu ʿām 2002. Wa-taʿmalu baladiyyāt Guarakasaba wa Kuritiba ka-marākiz li-riḥlat al-baḥth.
Unshūṭat al-mushāraka (R$ 1 500,00 fī dáfʿa wāḥida aw bi-l-taqsīṭ ḥattā 6 dáfʿāt) tushmúlu: al-naql (al-inṭilāq min Zhuis-di-Fora, Wilāyat Minas-Zherays, aw min ayyi nuqṭa ʿalā ṭarīq al-riḥla), al-iqāma fī al-funduq maʿa al-fuṭūr, al-mawādd al-dirāsiyya, tadhākir al-dukhūl, wa-shahāda rasmiyya bi-itbāʿ 100 sāʿatin min al-tadrīb al-naẓarī wa al-ʿamaliyya.

Al-Mu'tamar al-Waṭanī li-Usāqifat al-Barāzīl (CNBB)

Ilā al-ikhwa wa al-akhawāt fī al-abrashiyyāt wa al-rʿāyā wa al-mujtamaʿāt al-kanasiyya al-asāsiyya li-Kanīsatina al-Kāthūlīkiyya.
Fī iṭār usbūʿ al-iḥtifāl bi-ʿĪd al-Istiqlāl fī hādhā al-ʿām, sayuʿqad al-Istiſtā' al-Shaʿbī ḥawla qāḍīyat khakṣakha sharikat al-sikak al-ḥadīdiyya Estrada de Ferro Vale do Rio Doce, wa-kadhalika ḥawla ḥuqūq ijtimāʿiyya ukhrā taqūmu Kanīsatunā dā'iman bi-l-difāʿ ʿanhā.
Naḥnu, usāqifat al-Lajna al-Usqufiyya lil-ʿAmal al-Raʿawī fī Qāḍāyā al-Khayr wa al-ʿAdāla wa al-SalāM lil-CNBB — al-monsen'or Pedru Luis Stringini, al-monsen'or Zhoze Luis Ferreyra Salles, al-monsen'or Gil'yerme Antoniu Verlang, al-monsen'or Ladislau Bernyaski, al-monsen'or Demetriu Valentini wa al-monsen'or Maurisiu Grotto — bi-ism al-khidmāt al-raʿawiyya al-ijtimāʿiyya, nadʿū al-jamīʿ lil-mushāraka al-fāʿila fī hādhā al-Istiſtā'.

Inna ʿamaliyyat khakṣakhat al-sharika tūjahu al-ān iʿtirāḍāt qāḍā'iyya — la-qad tuquddima bi-akthar min 100 daʿwā taḍaʿu qānūniyyat al-mazād maḥalla shakk.
Wa-kamā ashāra qabla mazād 1997 al-marḥūm al-monsen'or Lusianu Mendes de Almeyda fī jalsat istimāʿ ʿāmmah, inna hādhihi al-khaṭwa kānat ghayra akhlāqiyya ʿalā al-itlaq.

— "Lā yajūzu bayʿu mu'assasatin mithla Vale do Rio Doce dūna iʿlām al-shaʿb bi-qīmatihā al-ḥaqīqiyya, wa-bi-ahammiyyatihā al-tārīkhiyya wa al-thaqāfiyya wa al-siyāsiyya..."
— "Lā yajūzu bayʿu arḍi al-waṭan nafsa-hā..."
— "Inna al-ṣafqa al-latī tamassu tharwat al-shaʿb... kāmilah lā yumkinu an tatim dūna iʿlām wa-taʿlīm wa-akhdh ra'yi al-shaʿb nafsihi".
Amanan fī al-mawqif al-fāʿil wa al-musāʿada min kulli akhin wa-ukht, nabʿathu lakum salāmanā al-akhawey al-dāfi'.

Al-Bayān al-Rasmī li-sharikat Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR)

Rio-de-Zhaneyro, 10 Māyū 2006 – PETRÓLEO BRASILEIRO S/A - PETROBRAS,
[Bovespa: PETR3/PETR4, NYSE: PBR/PBRA, Latibex: XPBR/XPBRA, BCBA: APBR/APBRA],
sharika brazīliyya lil-ṭāqa, tamassu nashāṭan duwaliyyan, tuʿlinu anna farʿahā Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR) qad nashara fī al-ṣiḥāfa al-maḥalliyya al-bayān al-tālī:
"Fīmā yaftariqu bi-taʿyīn mudarā' wa-shurakā' min al-mu'assasa al-ḥukūmiyya Yacimientos Petrolíferos Fiscales Bolivianos (YPFB) fī sharikat Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR), tuʿlinu al-idāra annahu li-dukhūl hādhihi al-qarārāt hayyiza al-tanfīdh, yajibu awwalan murāʿāt silsila min al-ijrā'āt al-qānūniyya al-muʿtabarah fī al-Dustūr al-Siyāsī lil-Dawla wa-qawānīn al-Jumhūriyya.
Tushmúlu hādhihi al-khaṭawāt, qabla kulli shay', ijrā' mufāwaḍāt bayna al-ṭarafayn ḥawla qīmat al-taʿwīḍ al-mālī al-ladhī tatʿahhadu YPFB bi-dafʿihi li-sharikat Petrobras fī muqābil ḥiṣṣa minal-aswāq fī PBR bi-nisbat 50% zā'id sahm wāḥid;
iṣdār qānūn khāṣṣ yuʿṭī YPFB al-ḥaqq al-rasmī li-takūna shārīkan fī PBR;
al-tasjīl al-qānūnī li-ḥiṣṣat al-aghlabiyya (50% zā'id sahm wāḥid) li-ṣāliḥ YPFB; ijrā' taʿdīlāt fī al-niẓām al-asāsī lil-sharika;
wa-kadhalika daʿwat al-Jamʿiyya al-ʿĀmmah lil-shurakā' wa-ijrā'āt ukhrā.

Wa-baynamā anā ash-taghilu bi-hādhā hunā, la-qad wa-ḍaʿtu bi-shaklin muwāzin ʿalā qāʿi dāt ku'ūs al-ḥalwā al-frawla (amorodo/morango) al-ṭāzaja al-muqaṭṭaʿah.
AL-MUTAKALLIM 2 Fī al-ijmāl sittumi'ah ghrām.
AL-MUTAKALLIM 1 Inna waṣfat al-brigadeiro hādhihi fī al-ku'ūs takūnu ghayra ʿādīya jiddan, antum dāmānan satubhishūna jamīʿ al-ḍuyūf. Unẓurī, al-ān sa-uḍīfu hunā thulth kūb shāy min khall al-balsāmīk (vinagre balsâmico).
AL-MUTAKALLIM 2 Jamʿu al-balsāmīk maʿa al-frawla — hādhā fantaṣtīkā faqaṭ.
AL-MUTAKALLIM 1 Bi-al-ṣarāḥa, anā fī al-sābiq lam... lam atadhawwaq aṭlaqan al-frawla maʿa khall al-balsāmīk. Wa-baʿda dhālik nushaddidu bi-al-sukkar.
AL-MUTAKALLIM 2 Niṣf kūb.
AL-MUTAKALLIM 1 Niṣf kūb minal-sukkar. Sa-amzujuhu bi-shaklin mustaqill likay yatashakkala sharāb dakhīn mutajānis minal-sukkar wa al-balsāmīk, wa-asubbuhu fawrān fī sittumi'ah ghrām minal-frawla al-latī qaṭṭaʿnāhā fī al-sābiq ilā qiṭaʿ.
