Limaadha al-‘amal bi-al-mashari’e?

Wadhdhih shukukak hawla kayfiyat istikhdaam manhajiyat al-mashari’e al-ta’leemiyyah fi al-fasl.

Al-khubaraa’ yadmanuun: al-‘amal ma’a al-mashari’e al-ta’leemiyyah raa’i’ wa mudeesh. Raa’i’ li-qudratihi ‘ala ishraak hatta al-tullaab al-akthar la-mubalaah. Mudeesh li-annahu yahmil fi tayaatihi budhoor ghayr al-mutawaqqa’. Fima yalee, sataqra’ tis’at as’ilah mutakarrirah hawla al-mawdoo’, yujeeb ‘alayha thalathat khubaraa’ kibaar. Hadhihi as’ilah tuqliq man lam yughaamir qattu fi hadha al-‘aalam – wa man laysa mubtadi’an ba’du. Duyoofuna hum Regina Scarpa, al-munassiqah al-‘aammah li-Markaz al-Diraasaat wa al-Tawtheeq lil-‘Amal al-Mujtama’ee fi San Bawlu; Vinicius Ítalo Signorelli, ustaadh al-‘Uloom al-Tabee’iyyah wa ‘udw barnamaj “Ma’ayeer al-‘Amal” al-taabi’ li-Wizarat al-Ta’leem; wa Vera Grellet, munassiqat al-mashari’e fi Redeensinar. Al-‘adeed min al-ijaabaat tukammil ba’daha al-ba’d. Wa ukhraa takshif ‘an wijhaat nazar mukhtalifah. Daleel ‘ala annahu fi al-ta’leem, kama ta’lam jayyidan, laa tujad haqeeqa wahidah mutaballurah.

Madha ya’nee al-‘amal ma’a al-mashari’e al-ta’leemiyyah?

Vera Grellet Huwa al-bad’ min as’ilah aw mawaaqif haqeeqiyyah wa mulmasah wa mawdi’iyyah tahumm al-tullaab haqqan. Fahm al-mawqif al-mushkilah huwa hadaf al-mashroo’. Tatimm munaaqashat wa takhteet al-ijra’aat wa al-ma’aarif al-lazimah lil-fahm bayna al-mu’allim wa al-tullaab. Likulli shakhs mahaam wa mas’ooliyyaat. Innahu mithl rihlah: nahnu fi Bakin (maa na’rifuhu) wa nureed al-dhahaab ila Shan-ghay (al-masaaru yumaththil maa sanata’allamuhu). Yajib an nuqarrir madha naf’al, wa madha na’khudh, wa nuwazzi’ al-mahaam. Fi al-tareeq, sanattakhidh ayḍan qaraaraat jadeedah.

Al-ta’theer al-thaani, al-qawi bishakl khaass li-Roosiya wa laakinnahu yu’athir ayḍan ‘ala al-Baraazeel, huwa anna al-azmah al-maaliyyah wa al-masrifiyyah addat ila inkhafaad haadd fi al-i’timaad lil-iqtisaadaat al-naashi’ah. Wa qad wada’a hadha daghṭan nuzooliyyan ‘ala ‘umlaat jamee’ al-buldaan taqreeban, allati idtarrat li-istikhdaam ihtiyaatiyyaatiha min al-‘umlaat al-ajnabiyyah li-ta’weed sahb al-tamweel al-masrifi al-dawli.
