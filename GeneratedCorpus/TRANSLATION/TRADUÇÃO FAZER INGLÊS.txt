The 1st Special Education Symposium will take place on August 19th and 20th.
Early bird registration is now open with a discount.

The Anália Franco campus will host the 1st Special Education Symposium - PROESP on August 19th and 20th, focusing on the theme: "Discussing Limits and Possibilities in Teaching Practices".
Aimed at professionals working in the school inclusion of students with disabilities and in special education research, the event will offer participants an opportunity to reflect on and debate contemporary issues. It will highlight the expertise of interdisciplinary teams, who build their knowledge based on teachers' practices and educational and/or specialist support to ensure quality education.
The Symposium is an initiative of the Special Education Program - PROESP, maintained by the Pro-Rectory of Extension and Community Affairs at Cruzeiro do Sul University. It aims to promote the integration of research, teaching, and extension in inclusive education, offering educational support to young people and adults with disabilities (intellectual, hearing, physical, and visual) and their families. This support facilitates their access to productive work, social, and cultural goods, while boosting their self-esteem and independence, and emphasizing the importance of their social integration.

21st TRANSLATOR'S WEEK - September 26th to 30th, 2011

Translator's Week is a traditional annual event held since 1981 at UNESP in São José do Rio Preto (SP), which brings together specialists, professionals, and translation students from various regions of the country.
In 2011, for its thirty-first edition, the Translator's Week at IBILCE – UNESP will center on the theme "Technology, Translation, and Culture". The goal is to demonstrate how indispensable various technological advances achieved since the late 20th century—such as translation memory software—have become to the profession. It also aims to deepen participants' knowledge of the essential tools every professional translator needs to master through intensive workshops running throughout the week.
The 31st Translator's Week will also extensively cover the cultural universe, featuring numerous short courses on literature, painting, music, cinema, and other arts, involving both foreign languages and the mother tongue.
Special focus will be placed on cultural processes and their relationship with translation, utilizing historical, linguistic, political, and social approaches.
We wish to highlight the importance of these two pillars in a translator's work: technical expertise and cultural background, both of which are indispensable in every single word they choose.

 Atlantic Forest Expedition
FIELD COURSE: Ecology and Conservation of the Atlantic Forest
(100 hours of theoretical and practical activities)
         13th Edition – July 21st to 31st, 2011
         REGISTRATION UNTIL: July 18th, 2011


Organisation: Green Brazil Group / Diaulas Abreu Foundation – IF Sudeste MG / Research Group on Planning and Management of Protected Natural Areas – IF Sudeste MG

The "Ecology and Conservation of the Atlantic Forest" course is a field program offered during the Atlantic Forest Expedition. It consists of an eleven-day journey through Conservation Units and other private, municipal, state, and federal protected natural areas, as well as research institutions in Paraná.
In addition to technical visits, classes and lectures are held, along with plenty of networking and discussions on ecology and conservation.
Activities are coordinated by Prof. Geraldo Majela Moraes Salvio, Professor of Ecology and Conservation Biology at the Federal Institute of Southeast Minas Gerais – Barbacena Campus, with the participation of other guest professors. The course is open to anyone interested in the subject, regardless of their background or origin.
There are 20 places available for Brazilian and foreign participants interested in learning more about the Atlantic Forest and issues related to its conservation, including the planning and management of protected natural areas.
Topics related to history, culture, traditional communities, community-based tourism, and sustainability will also be covered.
The course has run uninterruptedly every January and July since 2002, using the municipalities of Guaraqueçaba and Curitiba as operational bases.
The price (R$ 1,500.00 upfront or payable in up to six instalments) includes transport (departing from Juiz de Fora, MG, or any other location along the route), hotel accommodation with breakfast, teaching materials, entry fees to most visited sites, and a certificate for 100 hours of theoretical and practical activities.

Announcement from Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR)

Rio de Janeiro, May 10th, 2006 – PETRÓLEO BRASILEIRO S/A - PETROBRAS,
[Bovespa: PETR3/PETR4, NYSE: PBR/PBRA, Latibex: XPBR/XPBRA, BCBA: APBR/APBRA],
a Brazilian energy company with international operations, announces that its subsidiary Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR) released the following statement to the local press:
"Regarding the appointment of directors and syndics of Yacimientos Petrolíferos Fiscales Bolivianos (YPFB) within the company Petrobras Bolivia Refinación (PBR), the company clarifies that for these measures to become effective, a series of legal and corporate procedures and formalities must be previously fulfilled, in accordance with the Political Constitution of the State and the laws of the Republic.
These procedures consist mainly of negotiations between the parties regarding the financial consideration due by YPFB to Petrobras for the exchange of 50% plus 1 of the shares of PBR;
the enactment of a special law authorising YPFB's participation as a shareholder in PBR;
the formalisation of the transfer to YPFB of the majority share block of 50% plus 1; the amendment of the bylaws;
and the calling of a Shareholders' General Meeting, among others.
Furthermore, it is essential to modify Article 8 of the Privatisation Law No. 1330, which prohibits public companies from acquiring assets, securities, and other rights of companies transferred to the private sector under the protection of this legal instrument.
This and other key issues will be addressed tomorrow at a working meeting to be held in La Paz, with the presence of the Bolivian Minister of Hydrocarbons, Andrés Soliz Rada, the President of YPFB, Julio Alvarado, the Brazilian Minister of Mines and Energy, Silas Rondeau, and the President of Petrobras, José Sergio Gabrielli de Azevedo."

Hello everyone!
A year has already passed since the Brazil-Scotland Association was created, and this March, the elections for the new members of our Board and Coordinators will take place.

In the past, you could hardly ever see Brazilians on the streets of Edinburgh, whereas today, we run into one on every street corner. We estimate that there are currently around two thousand Brazilians living in Edinburgh. A comment we always hear is that we lack a little corner of our own, a meeting point for our entire community.
And that is exactly where the work of the Association comes in!

The Association was created for this exact purpose: to be a meeting point and a link for all Brazilians who are in Edinburgh, regardless of why they are here.
The structure is already in place and running at full steam at the Tollcross Community Centre.

Don't stay out of this democratic electoral process. After all, your vote and contribution are very important!
Spread the word to all the Brazilians you know.
We count on your presence!

MHNJB offers the extension course "Learn to Make, Assemble, and Install Your Low-Cost Solar Heater"

This March, the Museum of Natural History and Botanical Garden at UFMG (MHNJB) opens registrations for the extension course "Learn to Make, Assemble, and Install Your Low-Cost Solar Heater", which was a major success last year.
The course covers the entire process of building and installing residential solar heaters, with a key advantage: those enrolled leave the Museum fully capable of building their own equipment. They will learn theoretical concepts and then move on to practice, building a model heater together as a team. In addition, each participant will receive a comprehensive manual containing the sizing, full list of materials and necessary tools, as well as installation techniques, enabling them to easily apply the acquired knowledge at home.
The Low-Cost Solar Heater (ASBC) is a project developed by the Sociedade do Sol, an organisation based at the Center for Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Technology (CIETEC) in São Paulo.
Its objective is to democratise and spread the self-construction technology of solar water heaters (from 200 to 1,000 litres), designed to partially replace the electricity consumed by the 36 million Brazilian families who use electric showers in their houses and flats.
The practical workshop will take place on March 13th from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm, and registrations will be open from March 8th to 12th, 2010.
