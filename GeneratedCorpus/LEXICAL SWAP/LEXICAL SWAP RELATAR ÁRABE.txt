Limadha al-‘amal ma‘a al-mashari‘

Waddih shukukuka hawl kayfiyyat al-istikhdam fi al-fasl li-manhajiyyat al-mashari‘ al-ta‘limiyya

Al-khubara’ yadmanun: al-‘amal ma‘a al-mashari‘ al-ta‘limiyya huwa ra’i‘ wa mufaji’. Ra’i‘ bi-qudratihi ‘ala ishrak hatta al-tullab al-akthar ihmalan. Mufaji’ li-annahu yahmil fi tayyihi jirm al-ghayr al-mutawaqqa‘. Fima yali, anta sa-taqra’ tis‘ as’ila mutakarrira ‘ala al-mawdu‘, mujab ‘alayha min thalatha khibra kabira fi al-mawdu‘. Hiya as’ila tu‘adhdhibu man lam yughamir abadan fi hadha al-‘alam – wa aydan man laysa mubtadi’an ba‘d. Dudafana’ hum Regina Scarpa, al-munassaqa al-‘amma li-Markaz al-Dirasat wa al-Tawthiq li-l-‘Amal al-Mujtama‘i, fi San Bawlu; Vinicius Ítalo Signorelli, ustadh al-‘Ulum al-Tabi‘iyya wa ‘udw barnamij al-Mu‘ayyarat fi al-‘Amal, min Wizarat al-Tarbiya; wa Vera Grellet, munassaqat al-mashari‘ min Redeensinar. Katheer min al-ajwiba takmul ba‘duha al-ba‘d. Ukhrha takshif ‘an nuqat nazar mukhtalifa. Burhan fi anna, fi al-tarbiya, kama ta‘lamu jayyidan, laysa hunak haqiqa wahida mutaballura.

Madha ya‘ni al-‘amal ma‘a al-mashari‘ al-ta‘limiyya?

Vera Grellet Huwa al-inṭilaq min as’ila aw wada’i‘ haqiqiyya wa maddiyya, musawwaqa, tuhimu fi al-haqiqa al-tullab. Fahm al-wad‘-al-mushkila huwa hadaf al-mashru‘. Al-‘amalaat wa al-ma‘arif al-daruriyya li-l-fahm yatim ‘anha al-bahth wa al-takhtit bayn al-mudarris wa al-tullab. Al-jami‘ ‘indahum maham wa mas’uliyyat. Innahu ka-annahu rihla: nahnu fi San Bawlu (ma na‘rif) wa nuridu an nasila ila Salvador (al-tariq yurmiz ila ma sanata‘allam). ‘Alayna an naqrur ma naf‘al, ma nakhudh, naqsim al-maham. Athna’ al-rihla, sayakun ‘alayna aydan an nattakhidha qararat jadida.

Gustavo Kuerten, a‘bar sanam li-l-tinis al-brazilian, sayaṭil 30 sana hadha al-ahad. Kahadiyya, innahu yasta‘iddu li-l-‘awda ila al-mala‘ib. Batal li-Roland Garros thalath marrat wa raqm 1 al-sabiq fi al-‘alam, al-minṭaqi min Santa Catarina yastab‘id al-i‘tizal bisabab al-mashakil fi al-wirik wa yaḍa‘ khutaṭ li-l-‘awda ila al-dawra al-mihaniyya.

Wa ma‘a dhalika, Guga la yuhaddid tarikhan li-l-dukhul ila al-mal‘ab marra ukhra. Yufaḍḍilu al-hadhr wa yanwi an yu‘addida nafsahu jayyidan jasadiyyan wa nafsiyyan li-muwajahat rutinat al-bataliyyat wa al-asfar al-murhiqa.

Unzur asfal muqabala ma‘a la‘ib al-tinis wa, min al-an, fariq al-Mawqi‘ yu‘abbir ‘an raghbatihi fi al-hazz al-sa‘id li-Guga fi isti’naf masiratihi, wa bi-l-ighḍa ‘an al-nata’ij al-lati tuqbil, li-ya‘lama anna manasibahu ka-ism kabir lil-riyada al-brazilianiyya qad sjjila fi al-tarikh.

Guga, hal satataqada‘?

Kullu dhalika kadhib, wa takhmin akhar. Man yataba‘uni min qurb ya‘lamu anni ‘ala ṭariq mukhtalif jiddan ‘an dhalika. Kuntu qariban min al-‘awda mundhu shahrayn, ‘indama asibtu bi-tihab al-jirar.

Al-an, ana atadarrub kulla yawm wa yaqtaribu al-waqt li-l-bid’ bi-l-tafkir fi ba‘ḍ al-bataliyyat. Lakin aydan ufaḍḍilu an la uhaddida shay’an wa an abqa hadi’an li-a‘rif al-waqt alladhi akunu fihi wathiqan wa aminan bi-anni jahiz li-muwajahat al-bataliyyat.

Kayfa hiya rutinatu fi hadhihi al-marhala min al-niqah?

‘Adiyya. Astayqiz, adhhab li-l-tadrib, a‘mal al-‘ilaj al-natural, al-juz’ al-jasadi, wa qad bada’a ‘amaliyya al-dukhul ila al-liyaqa hata ba‘d asabu‘ aw shuhur bi-istan asabi‘a al-dukhul ila al-bataliyyat.

Kayfa satu’aththir al-azma al-maliyya ‘ala al-Brics?
Li-l-azma thalath ta’thirat wasi‘a, tu’aththir ‘ala al-jami‘, lakin bi-darajat mukhtalifa. Al-awwal huwa tabattu’ kabir fi numuww al-talab ‘ala al-muntajat al-musanna‘a, khususan fi al-Wilayat al-Muttaḥida wa Urubba, wa hadha sayakuun lahu ta’thir qawi ‘ala al-Sin. Al-ta’thir al-thani, alladhi aththar ‘ala Rusia bi-shakl khass qawi, lakin aydan ḍarab al-Brazil, huwa anna al-azma al-maliyya wa al-masrafiyya adat ila taqlis kabir fi al-‘i’timanat li-l-iqtiṣadat al-nashi’a. Hadha wallada ḍaghtan li-l-‘umlaat fi mu‘zam hadhihi al-buldan, wa tamam jami‘uha ‘alayha an tunfiq iḥtiyaṭiyyatiha li-ta‘wiḍ saḥb al-tamwil al-masrafi al-dawli. Waqa‘at Rusia fi wad‘ sayyi’ bi-khususiyya, bisabab al-madd alladhi iqtaḍa bihi al-qita‘ al-khas al-rusi min al-nizam al-mali al-dawli. Wa akhiran, hunak al-tadhabdhub al-kabir li-l-dawra al-iqtiṣadiyya al-‘alamiyya, alladhi sabab taṣḥiḥan kabiran fi si‘r al-sil‘a. Hadha sayakuun ta’thir ‘ala Rusia, bisabab al-bitrul, wa ‘ala al-Brazil, bisabab kham al-hadid wa kathir min al-sila‘ al-ukhra allati tuṣaddiruha al-Bilad.

Hal al-iḥtiyaṭiyyat al-brazilianiyya kafiya li-muwajahat hadhihi al-azma?
Al-iḥtiyaṭiyyat al-brazilianiyya akbar jawhariyan min duyuniha qasirat al-ajal. Al-Brazil ‘indaha al-miziyya anna, idafatan ila al-iḥtiyaṭiyyat, tatawaffaru al-an ‘ala wusul ila 30 milyar dular min al-Fed ‘ala khaṭ al-swap, wa muḥtamalan yumkinuhu an yaskhuba fi al-adat al-jadida li-l-tamwil qasir al-ajal li-Sunduq al-Naqd al-Dawli. Idhan al-Brazil ‘indaha 30 milyar ‘ala al-aqall, wa rubbama hata 50 milyar, fi i’timanat idafiyya.

Yakfi min al-sihr
V – Kutubuka taḥaddathat aqall fa aqall ‘an al-batiniyya. Hal ma zilta ta‘tabir nafsaka saḥiran?
C – Fikrat al-saḥir hiya akthar kathiran su’al tasawwur al-kawn. Innahu ṭariqa li-l-nazar ila al-‘alam wara’ al-haqiqa al-maddiyya. Lakin ‘indi kutub ‘adida la talmass al-sihr. Kutubi tataḥaddath ‘an masai’il falsafiyya.
V – Hal takun hadhihi nuqta al-mushtaraka baynaha, fi ra’yika?
C – Al-nuqta al-mushtaraka hiya shay’ yud‘a al-uslub. Min awwal kitab hatta al-an, ana hafaẓtu ‘ala uslubin mutlaqan mubashir, mahdhuf. Ana astamirru fi al-qaṭ‘, al-qaṭ‘, hata aṣul ila jawhar al-shay’. Fi al-bidaya, su’i tafsir dhalika. Zannu annahu shay’ saṭhi. Lakin hia hadhihi al-ṣifa allati tu‘ṭi kutubi wajhaha al-farid.
V – Ba‘d hadha al-najah, hal al-naqd ma zala yu‘diq al-haluka?
C – Ana mu’allif mustaqtib jiddan: al-nas yuḥibbunani aw yakrahanani. Ana muta‘awwid. Lakin al-naqd al-wahid alladhi aḥzanani lam yatta jih ilayya. Kana ‘indama qalu inna qari’akun na‘jun. La urid ta‘mim, lakin hunak fashiyya thaqafiyya fi al-bilad.
V – Hal tashuru bi-annaka muṭarad biha?
C – Aẓunnu anna al-muṭaradin biha hum jami‘ alladhina la yandarijun taḥta mi‘yar mu‘ayyan, wa huwa an yuharrim ma huwa ghayr ma‘qul wa ghayr mutah. Lakin, al-hamdu li-llah, hadha yanṭabiq ‘ala al-naqd faqaṭ, alladhi ‘azala nafsuhu ‘an al-waqi‘. Al-nas alladhina yaktubuna hadha al-naw‘ min al-ashya’ yabqawna fi burj ‘aji, bi-la an ya‘rifu ma yadur hawlahum. Yaḥsabuna annahum yukmituna al-afwah, wa anna al-jami‘ yasma‘una ma taquluhu.
