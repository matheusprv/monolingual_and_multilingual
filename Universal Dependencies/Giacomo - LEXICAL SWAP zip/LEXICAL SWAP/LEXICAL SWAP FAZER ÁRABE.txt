Hadhihi ahada 'ashar yawm musaafir bi-wuhdaat al-hifz wa manatiq tabi'iyya ukhra mahmuiyya khaasah, baladiyya, willaayaat wa fidiraaliyya, 'alaawatan 'ala mu'assasat buhuth — Paraná.
