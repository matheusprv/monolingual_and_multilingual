RE: Olive oil

PRODUCTION OF THE OIL

*oil of olive refined: Produced by refining of the oil virgin, that presents high acidity and incidence of defects to be eliminated in the refining. Can be mixed with the oil virgin.

*oil of olive virgin: Obtained by processes mechanical. Depending of the acidity of the product obtained, this oil can be classified as being of the type extra, virgin or common. The oil virgin presents acidity maximum of 2%.

*oil extra virgin: The oil cannot pass of 0.8% of acidity (acid oleic) nor present defects. The organ that them regulates and defines which defects are cataloged is the Council Oleic International (COI).

*oil of olive common: Is obtained of the mixture of the oil lampante, inadequate to the consumption, recycled by means of processes physical-chemical and its mixture with oil virgin and oil extra - virgin. The oil of olive common does not have regulation.

TIPS OF CONSUMPTION:
To the consume the product, is advisable that if check its acidity and date of validity.
